Leadership Reflection

What this session means for manufacturing leaders:

Manufacturing leaders don't need to become AI researchers, but they do need enough hands-on fluency to ask the right questions of the tools their teams will use. Trying all eight platforms side by side was less about picking a "winner" and more about building the vocabulary to evaluate vendor claims and internal proposals with some rigor.

The bigger implication is organizational: if leadership's own AI literacy lags behind the shop floor's, adoption decisions get made on marketing rather than fit. Readiness assessments like this one are a small but necessary first step before any AI transformation programme can be sequenced sensibly.
